AI & Machine Learning

The field moves faster than any one person can keep up with.

New papers daily. New models monthly. It's easy to spend all your time reading about the frontier and none of it building at it. This tribe is for researchers and engineers who want to stop tracking the field and start shipping in it — a model fine-tuned, a pipeline built, a paper's idea actually implemented.

Understanding the paper isn't the goal.Shipping the thing is.

Web Development

Every framework promises to be the one that finally makes it easy.

Meanwhile your portfolio site is still "in progress," your side project has three unfinished features, and you've rebuilt the same landing page twice in two different stacks just to try them out. This tribe is for developers who want fewer tutorials and more finished, shipped, real work — even if it's imperfect.

Done and live beats perfect and local.Every time.

Blockchain & Web3

Hype cycles come and go. Real builders stay through both.

This space has more noise than almost any other in tech — narratives, token launches, threads promising the next big unlock. This tribe is for the ones actually writing contracts, building protocols, and shipping products, who need people to build alongside who care about the tech more than the trend.

Anyone can post about web3.Few actually ship on it.

Cybersecurity

You're trained to think about what could go wrong. It can be isolating.

Certifications, labs, CTFs, threat research — the learning never really stops, and it's often solitary by nature. This tribe is for the defenders who want to build real skills in the open, alongside others doing the same unglamorous, high-stakes work.

Vigilance is easierwhen you're not doing it alone.

Cloud & Infrastructure

Nobody notices infrastructure until it breaks.

You're the layer everything else depends on — and the work is often invisible by design. This tribe is for cloud engineers and technicians who want recognition for the systems they build, and people around them who actually understand what "it just works" took to build.

Invisible work is stillwork worth showing.
