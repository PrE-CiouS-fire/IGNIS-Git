Execution Space

The heartbeat of IGNIS.

This is where goals get declared, progress gets tracked, and people hold each other to what they said they'd do. If you want structure and visible momentum more than anything else, this is home base.

Not another to-do list.A room that notices if you don't show up.

Founders Space

Building something is heavy. Building it alone is heavier.

This room is for the people carrying a product, a company, or a team — where the conversations are about real decisions, real trade-offs, and real weight, with peers who are carrying something just as real.

Fewer cheerleaders.More people who get it.

Finance Space

Skill without income is a hobby. Income without structure doesn't last.

This room is for builders figuring out how to actually get paid for what they know — pricing, offers, sustainable rates — without needing to become a full-time marketer to do it.

Your skill has a price.This is where you learn to name it.

Building Pods

Some things are better built with two or three people than one.

Building Pods are small teams formed inside IGNIS to build one real thing together — a project, a product, an experiment — with the same accountability as solo execution, but shared.

Not a team you hired.A team that showed up.

Impact Space

Skill is a tool. Where you point it is a choice.

This room is for builders who want their work to serve something beyond the next feature or the next dollar — real problems, real communities, real consequences. Ambition and conscience, in the same room.

Change the world.Not just your portfolio.

Pick a room. Show up.

Every Space has people already inside it, doing the work.
