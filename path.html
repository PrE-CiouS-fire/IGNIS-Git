The Path

Everyone starts somewhere.

IGNIS doesn't ask where you went to school or how long you've coded. It asks where you are right now — and meets you exactly there, with people a few steps ahead and a few steps behind.

01 — SELF-TAUGHT

Learning without a map

You taught yourself, and you're still figuring out what "good" looks like.

No bootcamp cohort, no professor, no built-in peer group — just you, documentation, and a lot of trial and error. That's a real strength. It's also lonely, and it's easy to wonder if you're actually progressing or just spinning.

Here, you'll:

Seehow others at your stage are actually building. Getreal feedback instead of guessing in the dark. Builda track record you can point to, not just a list of things learned.
02 — INTERMEDIATE

Skilled, but scattered

You can build things now. The problem isn't ability — it's direction.

You've got real skills and a handful of finished projects, but also a dozen half-finished ones, and no clear sense of what to focus on next. This is the stage where scattered energy either compounds into momentum, or quietly stalls out.

Here, you'll:

Focuson fewer things, finished properly. Learnfrom people slightly ahead of you, not just tutorials. Startbeing the one others come to for help.
03 — FOUNDER

Building while leading

You're not just writing code or research anymore — you're carrying a whole thing.

Product, team, users, runway, your own execution — all at once, and usually with fewer people around who understand the weight of it. This stage needs less teaching and more real peers who are carrying something just as heavy.

Here, you'll:

Findpeers building at your altitude, not just fans or followers. Tradereal decisions and blockers, not surface-level advice. Stayaccountable to shipping even when no one's checking on you.

Wherever you are, start here.

IGNIS meets you at your stage — not the one you wish you were at.
